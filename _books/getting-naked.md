This book was required reading when I joined Xpirit (Now Xebia). It describes three fears:

- Fear of Losing the Business
- Fear of Being Embarrassed
- Fear of Feeling Inferior

Safe to say I've seen a lot of "professionals" do some pretty weird stuff because of these fears.

The book resonates with me because so many of the things I loathe in business stem from these fears. My takeaway: no matter where you work, people would rather work with real humans than with PowerPoint decks and talk. Instead of telling a potential client what they will get if they hire you, just come out swinging and start helping them; the business will follow.
