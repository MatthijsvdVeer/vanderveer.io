I remember sitting by the water on a Finnish island, part of a wilderness trail. I was contemplating how I could possibly embed what I learned there in my very technical consulting job. When I told our guide [Kristian de la Riva](https://europeanwalkingsafari.com) about this, he told me about this book. I wrote it down and got a copy when I got home.

With all the tech bro's promising us "intelligent machines", this book is the best reminder that we're ignoring actual intelligence in the world, and more importantly, the non-human world. The parts we now refer to as "nature", as if intelligence is just a human trait. Enjoy!
