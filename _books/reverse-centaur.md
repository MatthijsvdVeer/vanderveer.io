In fiction, the mythical centaur is a powerful horse's body, with the addition of an intelligent human head (and torso). In technology, a centaur refers to a human using technology, being assisted by it. You're a centaur when you use a calculator. You're a centaur when you ride a bicycle. You remain in charge.

The book describes the inverse: a reverse centaur. This is where technology will do the thinking for you, using humans only when it can't complete the job. This is a warning sign by the way, not an invitation.
